REFLECTIONS · FROM EXPERIENCE TO INSIGHT

Pause, notice,
make a connection.

Reflection makes learning visible. It helps connect experiences to goals, recognize growth, and decide what to try next.

A REPEATABLE FRAMEWORK

Look back.
Look forward.

Use a reflection after a project, course milestone, workplace experience, or personal challenge. Keep confidential details out.

01

DESCRIBE

What happened?

What was the situation? What was I trying to do?

Keep it brief and factual.
02

NOTICE

What did I learn?

What worked, felt difficult, or surprised me? What skills or knowledge did I use?

Name evidence, not just a feeling.
03

CONNECT

Why does it matter?

How does this connect to a course, goal, value, or another experience?

Look for a relationship.
04

PLAN

What comes next?

What would I repeat, change, or explore next time?

Choose one practical next step.
